How to Redeem

  1. Reach Union Level 2 in the game.
  2. Open the in-game menu and go to Settings.
  3. Select Other Settings, then Redemption Box.
  4. Enter or paste a code and press Confirm.
  5. Check your in-game mail for rewards.

Important: Wuthering Waves codes can only be redeemed inside the game, not on a browser website.
